## 5. STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS
- **Zero-Volume Keywords:** Focus on keywords that show "Zero Volume" in tools like Ahrefs but appear in "People Also Ask" (PAA). These are often untapped gems for new consultants.
- **Topical Map:** Transition from ranking for "GST" (too broad) to "GST for [Audience]" (e.g., Exporters, Freelancers). This captures higher-intent traffic.
- **Tools SEO:** Optimize the calculators for "GST Calculator India 2026" to capture the frequent traffic from business owners checking their tax before filing.

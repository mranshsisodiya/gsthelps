Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026  |  gsthelps.com

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit gsthelps.com, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

When you fill out the contact form on our website, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (optional)
  • Your message or service inquiry

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Referring website or search query
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Respond to your service inquiries and provide GST consultation
  • Send you information about our services that you have requested
  • Improve our website and services based on usage patterns
  • Comply with legal obligations under Indian law

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

3. Contact Form & Third-Party Processing

Our contact form is processed through Web3Forms (web3forms.com), a third-party form service. When you submit the form, your data is transmitted to their servers to deliver your message to us. Please review Web3Forms' privacy policy for details on their data handling.

4. Cookies

Our website may use basic browser cookies to improve your experience (such as remembering your session). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you disable cookies in your browser, the website will still function normally.

If we integrate Google Analytics in the future, we will update this policy and provide an opt-out mechanism.

5. Google AdSense

We may display advertisements served by Google AdSense on this website. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

6. Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained for up to 12 months for business correspondence purposes, after which they are deleted. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal data. Our website is served over HTTPS (SSL/TLS encryption). However, no transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@gsthelps.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
